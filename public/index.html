Midnight Ninja

Choose a mode
Type a room code to play a friend, or press Quick Match to meet anyone online

Move with A and D or the arrow keys. Jump with W, Up or Space. Hold S or Down to crouch and slip through the low tunnel. Aim with the mouse and click to fire the sniper, or tap the screen on a phone. Press E to throw a bomb (you have 3); it explodes when it hits a target. Hold right click to levitate; it uses your energy bars, and they refill on the ground. Beat the blue-headband CPU on the right: it has a sniper, bombs and levitation just like you. Every 2 sniper hits on the CPU reload one bomb. Take it down once to win. A glowing barrier walls in the map: bullets stop at it instead of bouncing, and nothing can leave. Press Esc to return to the level menu.
